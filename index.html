How it works

Format & scoring

Assumptions used below — edit anything on this page directly (court times, results, players per match) to match your event. Team names & rosters live on the Teams & Squads page.

The Tie

Each of the 3 team-vs-team ties is contested across all 7 categories. First team to 4 category wins takes the tie. Remaining categories can still be played for the record, but the tie is already decided.

Each Match

Single set decides the match — no best-of-3. Rally-point scoring: MS to 15, WS to 11, WD to 15, MD & XD to 21 points.

League Table

2 points for a tie win, 0 for a loss. Ranked by points → head-to-head result → total category-match wins.
