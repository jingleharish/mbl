How it works

Format & scoring

Assumptions used below — edit anything on this page directly (court times, results, players per match) to match your event. Team names & rosters live on the Teams & Squads page.

The Tie

Each of the 6 team-vs-team ties is contested across all 5 categories. First team to 3 category wins takes the tie. Remaining categories can still be played for the record, but the tie is already decided.

Each Match

Single set decides the match — no best-of-3. Rally-point scoring: Singles (MS/WS) to 11 points, Doubles (MD/WD/XD) to 15 points.

League Table

2 points for a tie win, 0 for a loss. Ranked by points → head-to-head result → total category-match wins.

MS · Men's Singles MD · Men's Doubles WS · Women's Singles WD · Women's Doubles XD · Mixed Doubles
