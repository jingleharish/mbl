How it works

Format & scoring

Assumptions used below — edit anything on this page directly (court times, results, players per match) to match your event. Team names & rosters live on the Teams & Squads page.

The Tie

Each of the 3 team-vs-team ties is contested across all 7 categories. First team to 4 category wins takes the tie. Remaining categories can still be played for the record, but the tie is already decided.

Each Match

Single set decides the match — no best-of-3. Rally-point scoring: MS to 15, WS to 11, WD to 15, MD & XD to 21 points.

League Table

2 points for a tie win, 0 for a loss. Ranked by points first. If two teams are level, head-to-head decides it. If all three end up level on points (each team wins exactly one tie), head-to-head can't resolve a 3-way cycle, so it's decided by total category wins, then total points scored across every match, instead.

MS · Men's Singles MD1 · Men's Doubles 1 MD2 · Men's Doubles 2 WS · Women's Singles WD · Women's Doubles XD1 · Mixed Doubles 1 XD2 · Mixed Doubles 2
Squad note: each tie needs 12 player-slots — 1 MS, 2× MD (4 men), 1 WS, 1× WD (2 women), 2× XD (2 men + 2 women). With a 12-player squad split evenly by gender, that's every player turning out once per tie.
